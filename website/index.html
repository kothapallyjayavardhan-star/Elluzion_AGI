A CLEAR START

Start with your question.
Agree on the details.

What can I use today?

The separate buyer app is available. Store AI and the brand AI salesperson are planned products. The first offline hardware pilot is being prepared.

What does my store need to connect?

We’ll discuss the records available, their formats, your systems and the questions you want answered. Setup and integrations will be scoped for the pilot; POS connections are not yet confirmed.

What happens to our business data?

Before connecting data, we’ll agree on scope, access, processing location, retention and deletion. Those arrangements belong in the pilot agreement.

Can RFID tell you what a shopper is thinking?

No. RFID records tag activity. Distinguishing shopping interest from restocking or other movement remains an open engineering question.
